title: Learner profiles
---

These are design assumptions to test with participants, not observed audience data.

## Contributor without decision authority

Alex uses open lessons and sends corrections. They know collaborative work but have not set governance rules or administered a service. They want their feedback to reach someone who can act. They need a supplied case, distinctions between facts and assumptions, a text-only route, and permission to propose consultation without claiming authority. Assess their reasoning, not their institutional role.

## Maintainer facing handover

Sam edits a small open collection alongside other work. They know its users and publishing routine but have no formal budget or succession-planning training. They want a manageable handover and a clear support limit. They need examples that count review and documentation time, a capability test for a backup, and acceptable reduced-scope options. If Sam demonstrates strong prior knowledge, shorten the scaffold and ask them to justify an alternative using the same criteria.
