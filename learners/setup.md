---
title: Setup
---

No software installation, account, or coding is required. Bring paper and a pen, or a device with a plain text editor. The instructor supplies printed or saved episode instructions and the [project worksheet](project-worksheet.md), including the case, starter tables, service profiles, checkpoints, and decision record.

Open the worksheet from the learner menu before starting. For an offline session, print it and the learner all-in-one lesson using the browser's print command. Keep both: the worksheet holds the case inputs; the episodes hold the timed instructions and feedback. External references are optional reading and are not needed to complete a task.

You can write in a table, use labeled diagrams with equivalent evidence, or list each field as plain text. Color is never needed to convey meaning. Begin activities with silent writing, then compare in pairs; individual work is also possible. Tell the instructor if another format would help.

The fictional project is sufficient throughout. You do not need to disclose your own project or have authority to make institutional decisions. The proposed workshop is 120 minutes including a 10-minute break after governance.
